# LaunchGate 

### A Waitlist, Approval Queue & Guided Onboarding Funnel

Hey! Welcome to our project!

**LaunchGate** is a group project we worked on as part of our OJT.

The idea behind it came from a simple question: What happens when lots of people want to try a product before it's officially launched?

There needs to be some way to manage all those people, review their requests and decide who gets early access. We thought it would be interesting to build something around this idea, and that's how LaunchGate started.

### How does it work?

It's basically a journey from joining a waitlist to getting access to a product.

Someone signs up for the waitlist, the operations team reviews their request, and if they're approved, they receive an invite. They can then redeem that invite and go through a four-step onboarding checklist.

### What you'll find in our project

- A waitlist where people can sign up.
- An approval queue to review applications.
- Launch capacity management.
- A dashboard to keep track of the process.
- An invite system for approved users.
- A four-step onboarding checklist.

### Who are the users?

We have three different roles:

- **Prospect** – Someone who's interested in trying the product.
- **Operations Approver** – The person who reviews applications and handles approvals.
- **Founder** – The person who keeps track of the overall launch.
