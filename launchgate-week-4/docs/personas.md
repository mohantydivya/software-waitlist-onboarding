# Persona cards

These describe roles for the demo. They are working assumptions drawn from the PRD, not findings from interviews. Check them against the research in [product-notes.md](product-notes.md).

## Prospect

- **Responsibilities:** give accurate details, redeem the invite in time, and finish onboarding.
- **Goals:** get access, know where they stand, and reach first use quickly.
- **Common frustrations:** silence after signing up, not knowing their place in line, and invite emails that are vague or expire unnoticed.
- **Can:** join the waitlist, see their own status and position, redeem their own code, and complete their own onboarding steps.
- **Cannot:** see or act on another prospect, make decisions, or see metrics or the audit trail.
- **Preferred information:** current stage, queue position, the next action, and the code's expiry date.

## Operations Approver

- **Responsibilities:** review prospects oldest first, make consistent decisions, and record the reasons.
- **Goals:** clear the queue without over-admitting.
- **Common frustrations:** decisions scattered across spreadsheets and email, no view of seats left, and no record of why someone was refused.
- **Can:** start a review; approve, reject or hold; reissue expired codes; filter the queue; and read the audit trail.
- **Cannot:** set capacity, change the current batch, redeem a code, or reverse a final decision.
- **Preferred information:** seats left, prospect details, company size and use case, and the prospect's history.

## Founder

- **Responsibilities:** decide how many prospects the team can support, and watch how the launch is going.
- **Goals:** see conversion and drop-off without building spreadsheets, and match admission to what the team can support.
- **Common frustrations:** numbers that disagree, and no way to see where people drop out.
- **Can:** set capacity, choose the current batch, view the dashboard, and read the audit trail.
- **Cannot:** approve, reject or hold a prospect, or change a decision.
- **Preferred information:** funnel counts and conversion per stage, plus seats used and left per batch.
